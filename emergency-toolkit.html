⚡ Emergency Hack Toolkit

tap one, follow it, right now

Don't scroll and think — pick the first one that sounds doable and follow the steps. Every technique here takes under 5 minutes and needs nothing you don't already have.

How keyed-up or annoyed do you feel right now?
Physiological Sigh
Two quick inhales through your nose, one long exhale through your mouth.
~30 sec
▶
  1. 1Inhale deeply through your nose.
  2. 2Without exhaling, sip in a second small inhale through your nose on top of the first.
  3. 3Exhale slowly and fully through your mouth until your lungs feel empty.
  4. 4Repeat for up to 3 cycles.
30
Cold Water Reset
Cold on the face triggers a reflex that slows your heart rate fast.
~30 sec
▶
  1. 1Get to cold water, a cold pack, or even a cold can/bottle.
  2. 2Press it firmly against your cheeks and forehead, or splash your face.
  3. 3Hold for 15 to 30 seconds.
30
Butterfly Hug
Cross your arms, alternate tapping your shoulders — no device needed.
~2 min
▶
  1. 1Cross your arms over your chest, hands resting on opposite shoulders.
  2. 2Alternate gently tapping your left hand, then your right hand, at a slow steady pace.
  3. 3Keep going for 2 to 3 minutes.
120
5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
Name things you can see, feel, hear, smell, and taste.
~1 min
▶
  1. 1Name 5 things you can see around you.
  2. 2Name 4 things you can physically feel.
  3. 3Name 3 things you can hear.
  4. 4Name 2 things you can smell.
  5. 5Name 1 thing you can taste.
Grounding Object
Hold something textured and focus only on it.
~1 min
▶
  1. 1Grab any small object near you — a coin, your keys, a piece of fabric.
  2. 2Hold it and notice only its texture, weight, and temperature.
  3. 3When your mind wanders, gently bring it back to the object.
60
Count Backwards by 5s
Force your logical brain online — it can't spiral and count at the same time.
~1 min
▶
  1. 1Pick a number between 90 and 100.
  2. 2Count backwards from it by 5s out loud (100, 95, 90...).
  3. 3If you lose track, that's fine — just pick up and keep going for about a minute.
60
Categorize the Room
Sort everything you see into a category — same logical-brain trick, no counting required.
~1 min
▶
  1. 1Look around the room and pick a category: color, material, size, or shape.
  2. 2Silently sort everything you see into that category.
  3. 3Keep going until you've sorted at least 15 to 20 objects.
60
