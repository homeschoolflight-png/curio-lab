Hack the Launch

applied dopamine // hack zone experiment 04

Same system as before — but now you're driving it on purpose. Most tasks aren't actually hard once you're in them; the resistance is almost all bunched up right at the start. Here's how to get past it.

01 — THE PLAYBOOK

  • 1Shrink the ask. "Write the essay" has huge resistance. "Write one bad sentence" barely has any. The task doesn't actually get easier — you just stop needing a big deposit to start it.
  • 2Don't spend right before. A scroll session right before a hard task drains the exact reserve you needed to start it. Empty tank, big hill.
  • 3Put a small, certain reward at the end. It doesn't need to be big — it needs to be reliable. A known reward waiting at the finish line pulls you across the same way a predictable cue does.
  • 4Survive the first push, not the whole task. Resistance drops off fast once you're moving. The goal is never "get through the whole thing" — it's just "get past the first minute."
