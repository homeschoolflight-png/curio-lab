Before we get into how to use it, here's the short version of what dopamine actually is — and a simple mental model that makes the rest of it click.

01 — WHAT DOPAMINE ACTUALLY DOES

Dopamine isn't really the "feel good" chemical. It's more like your brain's motivation signal — it's what makes something feel worth the effort of going after.

It's why you can want something you don't even enjoy that much once you get it, and why a total lack of it — boredom — can make even easy things feel unbearable to start. Dopamine is less about pleasure and more about drive.

02 — THE DOPAMINE BANK

Picture your dopamine system like a bank account. Fast, easy, high-intensity hits — scrolling, sugar, games — are big withdrawals: a quick spike, then a dip below where you started, paid for later as low motivation, restlessness, or needing an even bigger hit next time. Slower, effortful stuff — a workout, real focus, a hard conversation — barely feels like anything in the moment, but it's a deposit: it raises what you have to work with later.
